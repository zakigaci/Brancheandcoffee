Our Story

A cozy welcome in the heart of Galway

Branch & Caffè is a cozy and welcoming café located in the heart of Galway, offering freshly brewed coffee, handcrafted sandwiches, and delicious pastries made with love.

Whether you're looking for a quick breakfast, a relaxing afternoon coffee, or a sweet treat, we've got something to satisfy every taste. We take pride in using high-quality ingredients, providing a warm atmosphere, and delivering friendly service that makes every visit feel like home.
